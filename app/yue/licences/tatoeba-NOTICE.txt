Tatoeba sentences in the Jiesuo Cantonese app
Source: https://tatoeba.org/ (Cantonese sentences with English translations, from Tatoeba's weekly exports,
https://downloads.tatoeba.org/exports/).
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France (CC BY 2.0 FR), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/

Each sentence is shown with its Cantonese text and its English translation. The contributor who wrote each
Cantonese sentence is named in app/yue/data/sentences.json (the fourth value of each item). The English text is the
translation Tatoeba links to that sentence; its contributor can be found on tatoeba.org. We added Jyutping to each
sentence ourselves (built from the app's word list) and changed nothing else.
