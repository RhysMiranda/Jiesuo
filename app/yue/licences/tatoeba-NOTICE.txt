Tatoeba sentences in the Jiesuo Cantonese app
Source: https://tatoeba.org/ (Cantonese sentences with English translations, from Tatoeba's weekly exports,
https://downloads.tatoeba.org/exports/).
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France (CC BY 2.0 FR), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/

Each sentence is shown with its Cantonese text and its English translation. Both are separate Tatoeba sentences and
both authors are named in app/yue/data/sentences.json: the fourth value of each item is the contributor of the
Cantonese sentence, the fifth the contributor of the English translation, the sixth the translation's Tatoeba id
(https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/<id>). Sentences whose translation has no named contributor are not shipped.
We added Jyutping to each Cantonese sentence ourselves (built from the app's word list) and changed nothing else.
